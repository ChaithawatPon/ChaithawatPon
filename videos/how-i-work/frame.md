# Workbench ledger

The repeated problem travels through four stamped checkpoints and leaves as a
small runnable system.

## Composition

- Focal element: a four-card work rail occupying the lower two-thirds.
- First anchor: `HOW I WORK` and Pon's one-line principle in the upper-left.
- Second anchor: a compact `RUNNABLE BY SOMEONE ELSE` receipt in the
  lower-right.
- Supporting detail: monospace step numbers, input/output metadata, a progress
  rule, and one active-state halo.
- Background: warm charcoal, a restrained amber radial glow, oversized ghost
  numerals, and a sparse workbench grid.
- Foreground: the progress rail and active checkpoint sit above all atmosphere.

## Motion

Use the `grid-card-assemble` blueprint. Cards assemble directly into their own
slots with `spring-pop-entrance`'s smooth settle. The amber active state steps
from OBSERVE to VERIFY to AUTOMATE to DOCUMENT, then the completion receipt
lands with a restrained `ambient-glow-bloom` halo. Everything clears back to
the bare workbench before the loop boundary.

## Constraints

- No gradients inside text.
- No generic app dashboard, browser chrome, cursor, logos, or photographic
  media.
- No bouncy overshoot.
- Keep all body copy at least 28 px at the 1920×1080 master size.
